# Vertactics product design

Read PROMPT and SPEC. Review the real editor or a current capture before changing product visuals or claims.

- Lead with a tactical decision involving elevation, sightlines, or an approach route. Use the same real terrain for comparable views and connect the example to an editor entry point. Choose the proof composition with [brand design](../../../skills/oddmash-brand-design/SKILL.md) and [UX design](../../../skills/oddmash-ux-design/SKILL.md); do not retain controls or views unless they help the tactical decision.
- Use actual editor captures or verified map data. Label approximate geometry and distinguish catalogue entries from editable terrain. Preserve the independent fan-project attribution.
- Use the documented dark-slate and lime identity and the current landing and editor JSON tokens. Keep mint for focus, pair team or terrain color with another signal, and verify Korean and English copy and label length.
- Test desktop and narrow workflows with keyboard and touch. Check that view toggles agree with visuals, captions, and pressed state. Verify the production base path, image and share metadata, and the main editor link.

For editor layout and tool interactions, use [editor design](../vertactics-editor-design/SKILL.md).
