# Oddmash product planning

Read the product brief, known constraints, and relevant repository agreements. Use the current screen or actual workflow as the starting point.

- State who arrives, what they need to do first, and what a successful result looks like. For a developer tool, show executable behavior. For a game, show a real play or planning decision. Keep claims tied to what exists.
- Identify entry points and behavior people already rely on. Separate product decisions from reusable standards and record any choice that would affect compatibility, data, or production.
- Define evidence before choosing a design. Use the same content, conditions, and measures when comparing alternatives. Distinguish observable results, user research, and hypotheses.
- Give each landing section one job. Lead with one realistic example and the smallest action that exposes the difference. Test whether someone can identify the difference on a narrow screen without reading the explanation. Put deeper controls, implementation details, and setup after that proof.
- Remove repeated product descriptions, generic claims, and links that duplicate the same next step without serving a distinct point in the flow.
- Capture the chosen direction, open decisions, and a reviewable first slice. For pages, include the proposed first action and the concrete evidence visible before secondary content.
