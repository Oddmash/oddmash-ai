# Oddmash logo design

Read the product brief, name, actual workflow, existing brand assets and intended placements. Preserve an approved mark unless a rebrand is authorized. Use [brand design](../oddmash-brand-design/SKILL.md) for the larger identity and [copyright and licensing](../oddmash-copyright-licensing/SKILL.md) for provenance and rights.

## Develop the mark

- Describe the product in one sentence and identify one visual idea that expresses its action. Sketch at least three materially distinct directions, such as a letter transformed by the product behavior, a geometric symbol or a purposeful negative-space mark. Compare their silhouette, memorability, meaning and differentiation without color or explanation.
- Study the approved [Boundra mark](https://github.com/Oddmash/boundra/blob/main/docs/assets/boundra-mark.svg): its angular B and diagonal opening suggest a boundary and crossing. Study [Juliya](https://oddmash.github.io/juliya/): its compact monochrome mark conveys line and fold geometry. Borrow the principle of a simple recognizable silhouette grounded in behavior, not their literal shapes or colors.
- Prefer an intentional silhouette, coherent proportions, aligned edges or purposeful optical compensation, and useful negative space. Use minimal paths and consistent corner language. Avoid generic shields, interchangeable tech hexagons, arbitrary initials, decorative gradients, stock icons and AI-generated detail that disappears at tiny sizes. A fold, overlap or tonal change must clarify the form, not substitute for it.
- Compare monochrome first, then introduce one product-specific accent where useful. Preserve a genuine single-color version. Distinguish mark from background container; do not bake a white square into every use unless specified by the target asset.
- Prepare a clean editable SVG with a viewBox, simple shapes, no external resources, no embedded raster and no text dependency for the mark. Derive any wordmark separately using licensed typography or original outlines. Use image generation for exploration when appropriate, then build or refine the actual vector geometry rather than tracing a blurry mockup. Check relevant trademark similarities and third-party asset rights; do not claim legal clearance.

## Symbol, wordmark and lockup

- Define the deliverables independently: the symbol is a recognizable non-text mark; the wordmark is the actual product name in a deliberate typographic form; a lockup combines them with fixed relative proportions. When both are requested, deliver separate masters as well as the combined placement that the product actually uses. Do not mistake a plain text label beside an icon for a finished wordmark.
- For a wordmark, select the actual name and script before drawing. Explore at least two typographic treatments and compare readability without a symbol. Preserve distinctive letter anatomy, counters, terminals, baseline and optical spacing. For Hangul and other complex scripts, inspect every syllable or glyph group at intended size; do not distort or omit strokes to imitate a Latin monogram. Use original vector outlines or documented licensed type, and convert deployment artwork to paths when reproducibility requires it. Retain an editable source.
- Establish a lockup grid: optical alignment, symbol-to-type scale, gap, clear space and minimum reproduced height. Test the name as the dominant readable element; a square symbol must not force the wordmark into an oversized header or become tiny beside it. Supply horizontal or stacked alternatives only for actual placements, not a generic asset checklist. Keep the symbol independently usable in favicons or compact app environments.
- Inspect the real header on both supported themes and at narrow widths. Keep the logo link accessible under the actual product name, avoid redundant announced names when the mark is paired with text, and use a meaningful text fallback if the asset cannot render. Preserve native link behavior and focus. Never bake a background into the standalone master solely to solve one surface; choose a contrast-correct variant or a purposeful app-icon container.
- Verify actual output assets, not a concept image: render a size ladder for the symbol, standalone wordmark and lockup; check legibility, counters, unequal weight, cropping and optical alignment. For a favicon, inspect the actual browser asset URL and rendered icon. Record evidence separately from legal clearance and structural build success.

## Acceptance

Render actual assets at 16, 24, 32, 48 and 128 px, on light and carbon surfaces and in monochrome. Inspect silhouette, negative space, small holes, clipping, optical centering, stroke weight, background transparency and contrast. Test favicon and app-icon masks or safe zones where applicable. Show the mark next to the product name, unrelated neighboring logos and an actual header, not just a large mockup. If a narrow detail vanishes, simplify geometry or supply an explicitly approved small-size variant.

Deliver the selected direction and reasons over alternatives, editable master SVG, appropriate light/dark or monochrome variants, favicon/app icon where required, and usage notes (clear space, minimum size, colors, prohibited distortions). Keep variant filenames kebab-case and verify actual rendered assets. Do not redefine a product's existing identity or create unnecessary export variants.
